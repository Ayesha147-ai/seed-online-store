Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on TrackSeed, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. When you apply to become an agent, we additionally collect your CNIC, business/agency name, and location details for verification purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Verify agent applications before approval
  • Communicate order updates and account notifications
  • Improve our platform and services

3. Payment Information

Payments made through our platform are processed securely. We do not store your full card or payment account details on our servers. Card payments are handled through our payment processor, and any manual payment confirmations (such as JazzCash) are verified by our team before order processing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared only with the relevant agent fulfilling your order, solely for the purpose of order delivery.

5. Account Security

Your password is stored using industry-standard encryption (hashing) and is never visible to our staff. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Cookies & Sessions

We use session cookies to keep you logged in while you browse the platform. These cookies are necessary for the site to function and do not track your activity across other websites.

7. Your Rights

You may update your account information at any time from your account settings. If you wish to delete your account or have questions about your data, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
